# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

In issues, discussions and any other project space:

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Keep to the subject: describe the problem, what you expected and what happened.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Use synthetic data. Never post real personal or company data, your own or anyone else's.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory or sexualised language or imagery, personal or
political attacks, publishing someone's private information without their permission, and any
conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and other contributions that break these
standards, and may block anyone who repeatedly or seriously breaks them.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/kevintu2007).
Reports are handled in confidence. Security vulnerabilities are reported as described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not here.
